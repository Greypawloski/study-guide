Commonly confused pairs

Twisting vs. ChurningTwisting = replacing with a different insurer through misrepresentation. Churning = replacing with the same insurer for commissions.
Moral vs. Morale hazardMoral = dishonesty. Morale = indifference ("I'm insured").
Peril vs. HazardPeril causes the loss. Hazard increases its chance.
Joint vs. SurvivorshipJoint pays at the first death. Survivorship pays at the second.
Fixed period vs. Fixed amountPeriod: time is set, the amount is calculated. Amount: payment is set, the time is calculated.
Extended term vs. Reduced paid-upExtended: same face, shorter time (term). Reduced paid-up: smaller face, lifetime (permanent).
Per stirpes vs. Per capitaStirpes = share passes to the bloodline. Capita = split among survivors.
Absolute vs. Collateral assignmentAbsolute = permanent full transfer. Collateral = temporary, to secure a loan.
Cross-purchase vs. EntityCross = owners insure each other. Entity = the business insures each owner.
General vs. Separate accountGeneral = fixed products, insurer bears risk. Separate = variable products, owner bears risk.
Annuitant vs. OwnerAnnuitant = measuring life. Owner = holds the rights.
Life free look vs. Annuity free look (AZ)Life: at least 10 days. Annuity: 10 days, 30 if 65+. Replacement: 30 days.
