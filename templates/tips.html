Test-taking strategy & exam day

How to approach the questions, and what to expect at the testing center.

Reading the questions

  • Watch for EXCEPT, NOT and ALL BUT. They flip the question. Rephrase it in your head as "which one is false?"
  • Find the key words: "first", "most", "best", "always". The exam often has two plausible answers, and the key word decides.
  • Answer before you look. Think of the answer, then find it among the choices.
  • Eliminate. Cross out obviously wrong choices mentally; your odds go up with each one.
  • Absolute words ("never", "always", "all") are often wrong in insurance, where most rules have exceptions.
  • Don't add facts. Answer based only on what the question says.

Managing time

  • You have 120 minutes for 100 questions: about 72 seconds each.
  • Do a first pass answering everything you're sure of. Mark the rest and come back.
  • Never leave a question blank; there's no penalty for guessing.
  • Change an answer only if you find a clear reason. First instincts are usually right.
  • Expect 5–10 unscored pretest questions mixed in. Don't let a strange one rattle you.

Before exam day

  • Schedule with PSI (Arizona's exam vendor). The exam fee is non-refundable, and you can test at a center or online with a remote proctor.
  • Score 85%+ on at least two full mock exams here first.
  • Review the Cheat Sheet the night before, then sleep.
  • Check the current PSI candidate bulletin for ID requirements and rules.

After you pass

  • You'll see your score on screen right away.
  • Complete fingerprinting if you haven't already.
  • Apply for your license through NIPR within 1 year.
  • Complete 4 hours of annuity training before selling annuities.
  • Track your 48 CE hours (6 ethics) over your 4-year license term.
