Can the owner change it?

Revocable vs. irrevocable

🔓

Revocable

The owner can change it any time, no permission needed. Most designations are revocable.

🔒

Irrevocable

The owner needs the beneficiary's consent to change them, take a loan, assign or surrender the policy.

One more rule

Minors can't collect directly

Proceeds for a child go through a trust, a court-appointed guardian, or a custodian (UTMA). Naming a trust as beneficiary avoids delays.
