Recap

Remember the trade-off

RULE: More guarantees = smaller payment. Life only pays the most; joint and survivor pays the least.
MEMORY TRICK: Whatever you choose is locked, so the other is calculated. Fixed period locks the time; fixed amount locks the payment.
