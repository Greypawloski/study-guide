Who owns the company?

Stock vs. mutual

📈 Stock company

Owned by stockholders (shareholders).

Customers buy policies, but don't own the company.

🤝 Mutual company

No stockholders. Owned by its policy owners.

Every policy owner is both a customer and an owner.

Both types are organized and incorporated under state law.

Demutualization

Demutualization: policyholders receive shares of stock in proportion to their gross premiums. It's often done to raise money by selling stock.

One more term

Mixed plan

An insurer that issues both participating and nonparticipating policies uses a mixed plan.
