The Policy Timeline

Free look, grace period, reinstatement and incontestability, with Arizona's numbers

Part 1

From delivery to a missed premium

10 days

Free look

Return the new policy for a full refund (30 days on replacements)

30 days

Grace period

Premium late but still covered. A death now pays the benefit minus the premium owed.

Lapse

Grace runs out

Automatic premium loan (if elected) or a nonforfeiture option kicks in

3 years

Reinstatement window

Evidence of insurability + back premiums with interest of no more than 6%. Not if surrendered for cash.

2 years

Incontestable

After 2 years in force, the insurer can't void it for misstatements on the application

2 years

Suicide exclusion ends

Measured from issue or reinstatement

Recap

Arizona numbers in order

10 free look → 30 grace → 3-year reinstatement at ≤ 6% → 2-year incontestable & suicide

Watch out: life grace is 30 days, while group conversion is 31 days. The exam likes to mix them up.
