Recap

Who owns the policies?

Cross-purchase

The owners insure each other.
Policies needed: n × (n − 1)

Entity

The business insures each owner.
Policies needed: n

TAX: Buy-sell premiums are not deductible; the death benefit is generally received income-tax free.
