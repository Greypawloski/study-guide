Section 7 · Federal Tax

1035 Exchanges

Swapping one contract for another without paying tax on the gain

Allowed or not?

Which swaps are tax-free

🛡️ Life insurance→🛡️ Life insurance✓ Tax-free
🛡️ Life insurance→💰 Annuity✓ Tax-free
💰 Annuity→💰 Annuity✓ Tax-free
💰 Annuity→🛡️ Life insurance✕ Not allowed
Life or annuity → a qualified long-term care policy also qualifies.

The fine print

Rules for a clean exchange

  • =Same owner, and the same insured or annuitant
  • $Any cash you receive ("boot") is taxable
  • !A 1035 exchange is still a replacement, so replacement rules apply

Memory trick

You can move toward an annuity,
never away from it.

WHY: Going from an annuity to life insurance would turn taxable annuity gains into a tax-free death benefit, so the IRS doesn't allow it.
