Once a MEC, always a MEC

What changes, and what doesn't

✓ Still the same

The death benefit is still generally income-tax free.

Cash value still grows tax-deferred.

✕ Living benefits get taxed

Loans and withdrawals come out gains first (LIFO), taxed as income.

Plus a 10% penalty before age 59½.

Example

A 45-year-old borrows from a MEC

Gain in the policy: $15,000

Loan taken: $10,000

Taxable as ordinary income: $10,000 (gains come out first)

10% penalty (under 59½): $1,000

Same loan from a non-MEC policy? No tax while the policy stays in force.

Exam clue: single premium whole life is usually a MEC.
