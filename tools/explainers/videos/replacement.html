Replacing a Policy
in Arizona

Who does what, and the deadlines to know

What counts

What is a replacement?

Buying a new policy or annuity where an existing one is lapsed, surrendered, converted, borrowed against, reduced or otherwise changed as part of the deal.

WHY IT'S REGULATED: Replacing can cost the client new surrender charges, a new contestable period and higher premiums at an older age.
